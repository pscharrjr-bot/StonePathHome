Site policy

This page explains how StonePathHome may handle basic usage information, cookies, and advertising-related technologies.

Privacy Policy

StonePathHome is designed as an educational mortgage-planning resource. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the site.

General use

StonePathHome is intended to work as a public educational website. You do not need to create an account to use the calculators or read the guides.

The calculator code runs in your browser and does not submit the values you enter to a separate calculator API.

Technical data

Like many websites, hosting providers, analytics tools, and related services may collect limited technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location derived from IP address, referring pages, and general usage patterns.

Cookies

StonePathHome and third-party services used by the site may use cookies or similar technologies to help the site function, understand traffic patterns, remember settings, measure usage, and support advertising features.

Advertising and third-party vendors

StonePathHome may display advertising now or in the future through third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense. These vendors may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads, limit repeated ads, measure performance, and improve ad relevance.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or to other websites.

Google AdSense

If Google AdSense is used on StonePathHome, Google may use advertising cookies to help serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage personalized advertising in Google My Ad Center.

Personalized and non-personalized ads

Depending on user settings, region, and platform behavior, ads shown on StonePathHome may be personalized or non-personalized. Non-personalized ads may still use contextual signals and limited technical information to deliver and measure advertising.

Email signups

The free checklist signup form is provided by MailerLite. Information submitted through that form is sent to MailerLite to process the signup and manage related StonePathHome emails. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link included in them. See MailerLite’s privacy policy.

Payments

The First-Time Home Buyer Survival Guide checkout is hosted by Stripe. Information entered during checkout is processed by Stripe; StonePathHome may receive order details needed to confirm the purchase, provide the download, and respond to support requests. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

If you contact StonePathHome by email, information you provide may be used to respond, review a reported issue, correct content, or improve the website experience.

Future features and policy updates

Third-party services and site features may change. This policy will be revised when those changes materially affect how information is collected or used.
